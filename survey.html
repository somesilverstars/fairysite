Oxford Academic: Fairytale Historical Context

We've talked about the stories themselves, but not yet about the context of which it is told. That is important too, as the exigence of these tales really helps develop depth and understanding of what times were like back then. Certain variations have different messages, characters, and endings. All of these are direct reflections of the circumstances of which these tales are told in.
